# Permissive Licenses

Use the exact variant; BSD-2-Clause and BSD-3-Clause are not interchangeable.

- MIT/ISC: short copyright permissions with attribution/disclaimer conditions.
  No express patent grant clause does not establish the absence of every possible
  implied patent right.
- BSD-3-Clause: also includes a non-endorsement condition; do not replace a
  dependency's license or drop its notices when distributing it.
- Apache-2.0: express copyright/patent provisions, defined patent termination,
  modification notices and applicable NOTICE requirements. It is not blanket
  patent protection or a trademark grant.
- MulanPSL-2.0: use the bundled official bilingual reference when relevant. It has
  its own scope/terms; do not equate it with Apache solely because both discuss patents.
